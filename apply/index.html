Apply for Tablet Benefit Options Safely

This page does not approve applications. It helps you prepare before you submit through a participating provider.

Before you apply

  1. Check your eligibility pathway first.
  2. Confirm provider availability in your ZIP code.
  3. Gather required identity, address, and benefit or income documents.
  4. Compare terms such as device availability, shipping timeline, and plan conditions.
