Editorial Policy and Content Standards

Our policy is built on accuracy, transparent updates, and realistic guidance.

Fact-checking process

We review official program documentation, provider terms, and published policy updates. We compare multiple sources before changing key claims.

Update process

When terms or eligibility details change, we revise affected pages and reflect update timing on core pages where relevant.

Program-change handling

We clearly note major status changes, including that ACP ended on June 1, 2024. We avoid stale language that suggests unavailable benefits are active.

No fake guarantees

We do not claim guaranteed approval, guaranteed devices, or universal offers. We state that outcomes depend on provider rules, ZIP code, inventory, eligibility, and current terms.
