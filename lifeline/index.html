Lifeline Program Guide and Reality Check

Lifeline is active, and it mainly provides service discounts for qualifying households.

What Lifeline usually covers

Lifeline support commonly applies to monthly service costs. Device offers may appear through some providers, but those offers are not universal.

What Lifeline does not guarantee

  • It does not guarantee a free tablet for every applicant.
  • It does not guarantee the same offer in every ZIP code.
  • It does not lock one fixed device model across all providers.

Current program context

ACP ended on June 1, 2024. Always verify current provider terms and local availability.
