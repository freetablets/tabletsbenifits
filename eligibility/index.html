Eligibility Pathways for Tablet Benefit Options

Most pathways are income based or program based. Documentation quality and matching information are important.

Income based pathway

Some providers review household income relative to current thresholds. Keep documents recent and readable.

Program based pathway

Participation in programs such as SNAP or Medicaid may support eligibility depending on provider terms and current rules.

Important context

  • ACP ended on June 1, 2024.
  • Lifeline is active and mainly supports service discounts.
  • Device offers vary by ZIP code and inventory.
