# Image Prompts

Use these prompts to generate premium editorial images for homepage and post content.

## Global Rules
- Featured images: no text overlay unless specifically needed for composition.
- Infographics: clean readable text is allowed.
- No fake government seals or official .gov badges.
- No provider logos unless legally safe.
- Visual style: realistic tablets, phones, paperwork, eligibility checklist, low-income benefit context.
- Color direction: blue, teal, white, light gray, small amber accent.
